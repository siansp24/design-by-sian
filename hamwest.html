Hamilton West School

2024/2025 5 weeks Web design

“Throughout the project I was impressed by her determination to create a website that was fit for purpose. Her attention to detail was outstanding, which resulted in a much abbreviated and easily read product which was a key goal of the contract. I am pleased to report Sian met our project aims and I recommend her for any future contracts or positions.”

— Mark Penman, Hamilton West School Principal 2025

The problem

↓

The school's original website was outdated and difficult to navigate. Important information was hard to find, and the website no longer reflected the vibrant, welcoming identity of the school.

The impact

↓

An accessible and easy-to-use website is essential for schools—it helps parents stay informed, makes finding information smoother, and represents the school's values and culture online. A poor user experience can lead to confusion, frustration, and missed information for families and staff.

The solution

↓

I designed a fresh, simple layout with clear navigation, improved readability, and a visual style that felt warm and welcoming. Key content like term dates, newsletters, and contact information was made easily accessible via the quick links section. I managed the entire project independently, coordinating meetings with the school board.

The result

↓

The school board and Principal was thrilled with the result. The final website received positive feedback from both staff and parents, and it's now a much more effective information too. It was a proud milestone in my design journey and gave me valuable experience working with my second real-world client and managing a project from start to finish.

User research

Key insights discovered after speaking with the school community, understanding what were common challenges.

Key updates/changes

Simplify navigation menu and group key sub-pages

Create quick links on home page for faster access to important information

Structure pages and create clear sections to reduce overwhelm of text content

Update and mondernise website design

Creating a site architecture

Grouped key sub pages below one navigation menu item for easier navigation to relevent content. Used simple phrases to represent what information users are looking for.

Flow path

Gathered common user journeys and mapped out how that journey would look using the site architecture.

Low-fi

Established a layout and overall structure using the site architecture. Focused on creating clear sections using hierarchy in text following a logical structure.

Original design

The school’s original website was outdated and lacked user-friendliness, making it clear that a refresh was necessary for them.

Updated design

The final stage was creating the new website design, which was handed over to the web developer. This involved user testing, applying feedback, and final approval from the Principal and School Board. The new design is fresh, simple, and easy to navigate.
