DefendHER

2024 6 weeks UI/UX & logo design

The problem

↓

There have been countless incidents of harassment, assault, and violence against women in both public and private spaces. As a result, many women feel the constant need to stay alert and aware of their surroundings, which can be mentally and emotionally exhausting.

The impact

↓

This ongoing sense of caution can take a toll on women's mental, emotional, and physical wellbeing. That's why there's a growing need for a reliable, empowering safety solution that truly supports and protects women in their everyday lives.

The solution

↓

We created DefendHER, a personal safety product designed for young women, empowering them to feel more confident and secure in vulnerable situations. The solution includes a durable, easy-to-access safety device and a connected app that instantly sends alerts to trusted contacts when activated. While an Industrial Designer developed the physical device, I led the design of the app interface and the branding process — from logo design to visual identity — ensuring everything resonated with our target audience.

The result

↓

Through this project, I learned a lot about designing for real-world impact. It challenged me to think deeply about user needs, accessibility, and emotional safety. It also pushed me to lead a branding process with empathy and intention — something I aim to carry into every future project.
